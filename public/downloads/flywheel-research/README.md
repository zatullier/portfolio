# Orion Flywheel Exercise Device research pack

Compiled September 13, 2026 for Zachary Tullier's engineering portfolio.

This archive gathers the strongest publicly available material found about the compact, unpowered Orion Flywheel (OFW) that flew on NASA's Artemis II mission. The catalog also includes a small, clearly labeled group of earlier ROCKY references because they document the Orion exercise-system problem and design lineage; ROCKY is not presented as the configuration flown on Artemis II.

## Contents

- `SOURCES.md` - readable, annotated index of every collected link.
- `sources.csv` - the same source inventory in a sortable format.
- `archive-manifest.json` - machine-readable inventory of files included in this ZIP.
- `LICENSES.md` - provenance and reuse notes.
- `documents/` - seven NASA/NTRS source files marked public, public-use permitted, or drawn from U.S. government publications.
- `media/orion-flywheel-exercise-device.jpg` - NASA public-domain hardware image.

The large Artemis II Reference Guide and JSC annual report remain direct links in the catalog. The focused technical publications are included in full.

## What is link-only

Commercial reporting, journal pages, third-party videos, discussion threads, and Canadian Space Agency media are cataloged as links and are not copied into the ZIP. NASA's 23.5 MB public-domain b-roll remains a direct NASA/Commons link for the same reason. This keeps the package useful without republishing material whose reuse terms vary or unnecessarily duplicating large media.

## Starting points

1. NASA's current `Crew Systems` reference is the best flown-hardware overview.
2. `I Am Artemis: Ryan Schulte` is the best people-and-development story.
3. NASA's Flight Day 2 update documents the first in-flight use and vehicle monitoring.
4. The 2026 NTRS comparison presentation is the strongest public mechanical characterization.
5. NASA's `From Friction to Flywheel` newsletter gives the clearest Apollo-to-Artemis visual history.

Links were checked during compilation, but external URLs and page contents can change.
