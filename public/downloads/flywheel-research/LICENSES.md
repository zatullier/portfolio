# Provenance and reuse notes

This file records why a source was copied into the archive. It is not legal advice.

## NASA material

NASA-authored material is generally not subject to copyright in the United States unless a page or asset states otherwise. NASA identifiers, logos, and third-party material appearing on NASA sites can have separate restrictions. See NASA's official media usage guidelines: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

The NASA/NTRS research records copied here are identified by NTRS as public and/or `Public Use Permitted`. Their original citation pages and full-source URLs are retained in `SOURCES.md` and `sources.csv`.

## Public-domain image

`media/orion-flywheel-exercise-device.jpg` is credited to NASA and is identified as public domain on its Wikimedia Commons file-description page:

https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flywheel_exercise_device_of_Orion_spacecraft.jpg

## Link-only material

Canadian Space Agency media, commercial reporting, academic publisher pages, third-party videos, and public discussion pages are not redistributed. The catalog links to the publisher's copy so that its own terms, attribution, and access controls remain in effect.

## Large link-only publications

The Artemis II Reference Guide and JSC 2024 Annual Report contain relevant flywheel pages but are intentionally kept as links because the full files are large and the archive's focused technical documents already preserve the substantive public record.
